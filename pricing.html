Questions before you upgrade

How does upgrading work?

Select Upgrade in Orbi (next to Settings) or on this page. Sign in with Google, pick Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime, and pay. As soon as PayPal or Razorpay confirms the payment, your plan is updated and Orbi unlocks Pro within seconds. There's no key to copy.

What happens when my plan ends?

Orbi shows your end date in the Upgrade panel. When it passes, Orbi switches back to Free on its own. Nothing is deleted: your extra spaces stay saved and unlock again the moment you renew.

Does it renew automatically?

No. Monthly gives you 30 days and Yearly gives you 365 days. Buy again any time to add more time on top of what's left.

Why is tax added?

Many countries require tax on digital purchases, such as 18% GST in India or VAT in Europe and the UK. We show the exact amount on the checkout page before you pay.

What does signing in share?

Only your Google name, email, and profile picture, used for your plan and receipts. Your spaces, tabs, shortcuts, and notes stay in your browser.

Which payment option should I pick?

PayPal works worldwide with your PayPal balance or any card. In India you can also pay in rupees with UPI, RuPay, cards, or netbanking through Razorpay.
