Clean up leftover tab groups

Chrome saves a copy of every tab group to the bookmarks bar (for example "Personal · 4"). Chrome keeps these, not Orbi, so they stay after removal. To clear them:

  1. Hide them all at once: open Chrome Settings → Appearance and turn off Show tab groups in bookmarks bar.
  2. Or delete them one by one: right-click a group in the bookmarks bar → Delete group.
